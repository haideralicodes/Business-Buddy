# Examples Catalog

One entry per screenshot in `landing page examples/`. Use this to decide which references fit the current project, then open those images.

## 01 — Slash (dark fintech, floating UI cards)
`01-slash-dark-fintech-floating-ui-cards.png`
- **Theme:** Near-black with soft vignette; top announcement bar ("Announcing our $XXM series B | Learn more →").
- **Type:** Large elegant display serif, white, 2 lines, centered. Inline glossy dark tile with a yellow ⚡ icon placed between words.
- **Layout:** Centered hero surrounded by scattered, slightly faded floating product cards (currency converter, APY chart, multi-entity switcher, virtual card stack, ACH timeline, transactions list, cashback feed). Cards fade toward edges so the headline stays dominant.
- **CTAs:** White pill "Contact Sales" + dark pill "Open Account".
- **Take from it:** Surrounding the headline with realistic product-UI fragments for depth and credibility.

## 09 — Lumina (dark blue AI, serif italic mix)
`09-lumina-dark-blue-serif-italic-ai.png`

- **Nav:** Tiny uppercase monospace links, centered; hamburger right.
- **Type:** Headline mixes regular sans with *italic serif* on key phrases, pale blue tint. Mono uppercase badge "■ NEW: …".

- **Below:** Centered statement text, a glossy 3D logo tile with a thin glowing vertical line connecting down to a bordered grid section ("■ WHAT WE DO") with two-tone text (white lead phrase, gray continuation).
- **Take from it:** Technical, editorial AI aesthetic; connector lines and bordered grid sections.
